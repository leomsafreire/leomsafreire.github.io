I'm Head of Football Analytics at <a href="https://atletico.com.br">Clube Atlético Mineiro</a>, where I lead the department responsible for the club's football data — from raw ingestion to advanced reporting. We work with granular, high-resolution datasets alongside data generated by the club's own processes, building in-house models and frameworks that support recruitment, strategy, and performance analysis.

I'm also an MSc student in Computer Science at <a href="https://ufmg.br">UFMG</a> and a researcher at the <a href="https://salabufmg.github.io">Sports Analytics Lab</a>. My research is on action valuation in football — what event data can and cannot tell us about the value of a decision on the pitch. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Pure Mathematics, also from UFMG.
