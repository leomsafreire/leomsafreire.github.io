I'm Head of Football Analytics at <a href="https://atletico.com.br">Clube Atlético Mineiro</a>, leading the club's Football Analytics Department — the pioneer dedicated football data and analytics department in Brazilian football. We integrate event, tracking, scouting, contract, transfer, injury, and club-generated data to support decision-making across the football operation.

Through proprietary models and frameworks, we inform decisions ranging from strategic planning and recruitment to performance and match analysis.

I'm also an MSc student in Computer Science at <a href="https://ufmg.br">UFMG</a> and a researcher at the <a href="https://salabufmg.github.io">Sports Analytics Lab</a>. My research is on action valuation in football — what event data can and cannot tell us about the value of a decision on the pitch. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Pure Mathematics, also from UFMG.
