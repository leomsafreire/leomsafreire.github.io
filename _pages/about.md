I'm Head of Football Analytics at <a href="https://atletico.com.br">Clube Atlético Mineiro</a>, leading the pioneering data and analytics department in Brazilian football. We integrate event, tracking, scouting, contract, transfer, and injury data alongside data generated by the club itself, feeding proprietary models and frameworks that inform decisions across the football operation, from strategic planning and recruitment to performance and match analysis.

I'm also an MSc student in Computer Science at <a href="https://ufmg.br">UFMG</a> and a researcher at the <a href="https://salabufmg.github.io">Sports Analytics Lab</a>. My research is on action valuation in football through goal probability estimation, comparing what event and tracking data can each tell us. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Pure Mathematics, also from UFMG.
